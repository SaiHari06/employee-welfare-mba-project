# Employee Welfare – MBA Project

## Overview

This project presents an academic study of **employee welfare practices** and their impact on employee satisfaction and workplace experience.

The study combines Human Resource Management concepts with primary data analysis to understand employee perceptions of welfare practices and identify areas for improvement.

## Objectives

* To study the employee welfare practices followed by the organization.
* To understand employee satisfaction with existing welfare facilities.
* To identify factors influencing employee satisfaction.
* To analyse the relationship between employee welfare and selected employee factors.
* To provide practical suggestions for improving employee welfare practices.

## Research Methodology

**Research Design:** Descriptive Research

**Data Collection:** Structured Questionnaire

**Sample Size:** 200 Employees

**Sampling Method:** Questionnaire-based survey

**Data Analysis:** Statistical analysis and interpretation of collected responses.

## Statistical Tools

The study includes:

* Percentage Analysis
* Chi-Square Test
* ANOVA
* Regression Analysis
